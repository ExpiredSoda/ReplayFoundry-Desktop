Replay Foundry Grounded Editorial Rephrase Policy 2.10

Purpose

This policy separates transport validity from audience-copy quality. A complete
XGrammar-constrained metadata object is retained even when a semantic copy rule
needs human review. One bounded rephrase may improve that draft, but rejection
of the rephrase never discards the source metadata or generated clip.

Structural rerolls

- A user reroll selects a different qualified variant intent from the same
  immutable evidence and typed authority.
- The rephraser changes the narrative lens, title opening and syntax, clause
  order, and description sentence plan. A synonym-only substitution is not a
  valid reroll.
- The new structure may use only facts supported by the same typed authority.
  It cannot add identity, emotion, intent, cause, outcome, dialogue, interface
  attribution, readable text, or game-specific event detail.
- Tags, grounding, and temporalVoice remain immutable except for the existing
  bounded non-English tag omission rule.

Typed editorial framing

- A host-validated StoryShapeOnly frame may change narrative organization and
  emphasis without becoming factual authority.
- The host derives one compact GrammaticalShapeOnly audienceFrame from the
  validated moment kind, primary presentation kind, creator authority, and safe
  nonhuman primary subjects that also participate in a retained primary action.
  It adds no scene fact. Its center kind controls the title's grammatical
  subject and the description opening.
- The validated moment kind selects the story role. The primary presentation
  kind selects how that role is presented. Supporting presentation kinds may
  supply secondary chronology, but cannot replace the primary grammatical
  center with another event or with an inventory.
- The grammatical center of the title and the opening description clause must
  be the supported presentation, review, decision, progress, action, discovery,
  complication, or outcome rather than a generic actor, camera, screen, prop,
  or displayed-content inventory.
- Cinematic sequences and in-world recordings are framed by their supported
  story or presentation role rather than by an inventory of visible people and
  props. Their typed rephrase authority retains every sanitized primary action
  for subordinate description detail and never duplicates those actions in the
  chronological progression.
- Candidate subjects are article-normalized, action-linked noun phrases. They
  may organize audience copy but cannot add factual authority, and the writer
  may omit them when none provides a useful editorial center.
- Narrative presentations use a compositional grammar with a presentation
  subject or locator followed by a retrospective framing predicate or literal
  action. The participant, action, and object remain factual subordinate detail;
  a generic actor cannot replace the presentation anchor.
- Retrospective-voice correction is subordinate to the audienceFrame. It may
  change the tense of a supported action but cannot replace a required cinematic
  or recording anchor with an actor-led action opening.
- Incidental clothing, camera inventory, display inventory, unstable readable
  text, and generic human subject objects are omitted from typed rephrase
  authority. Their omission does not change the complete grounding retained for
  validation and Studio review.
- Documents, objectives, menus, decisions, discoveries, actions,
  complications, and outcomes retain their supported moment role across polish
  and structural rerolls.
- Interactive gameplay centers the supported controlled action, objective
  progress, choice, or visible result. It never uses player, character, or a
  generic person as a substitute for missing creator authority.
- A rephrase produced under a useful validated frame is accepted only when the
  deterministic frame-adherence gate confirms that it retained the supported
  presentation and moment role.
- Frame adherence rejects observer-led openings and camera or display
  inventory, while permitting a subordinate neutral actor detail after a
  supported story-led grammatical center. It never requires exact premise
  vocabulary, so a natural synonym cannot fail merely because it paraphrased
  the StoryShapeOnly frame.
- Present-tense source observations and their equivalent retrospective audience
  forms are action-entailment equivalents. Audience copy must still satisfy the
  retrospective grammar gate.
- Repeated readable text may provide a bounded document, objective, interface,
  or in-world-message name. Single-window OCR remains excluded from typed
  rephrase authority.
- The factual title scope follows the host-validated supporting draft ordinals.
  Drafts outside that scope may add only chronological description context and
  cannot strengthen the title event.
- Unselected GeneralContext passages are omitted from rephrase authority. Only
  confirmed identity and already selected, clip-bound knowledge can influence
  wording during this pass.
- When otherwise equivalent drafts compete, a complete supported Latin-script
  action is preferred over a truncated, dangling, or non-Latin action.
- A missing or invalid premise falls back to the strongest supported literal
  beat and never causes generation to fail.

Review-required copy

- A schema-valid source rejected by an audience-copy validator is labeled
  ReviewRequiredMetadata and carries its typed review code.
- The rephraser may correct that code. If it succeeds, the review flag clears.
- When a source carries more than one review code, every applicable mandatory
  correction is composed into the same bounded rephrase request.
- Audience copy rejected for UnstableReadableTextReuse is withheld from the
  rephrase request. Omitted readable wording is non-evidence and cannot be
  reconstructed, corrected, translated, or paraphrased.
- Only an exact entry in typedAuthority.stableReadableText may be reused, and it
  remains subordinate to the supported presentation or event. When that list is
  empty, the rephrase uses no readable wording.
- If it fails, repeats the source, remains too similar to prior copy, or drifts
  from a useful validated frame, Replay Foundry retains the complete source and
  exposes its review issue in Studio and Publish. No additional automatic model
  retry is authorized.

Preserved bounds

- One batch-one greedy XGrammar-constrained text-only rephrase, seed 0, beam
  count 1, offloaded KV cache, and the 768-token ceiling remain.
- No video rerun, perception rerun, knowledge retrieval, unconstrained
  fallback, programmatic semantic repair, or hidden retry is introduced.
- Policies 1.0 through 2.3 remain readable under their original contracts.


Attributed automatic commentary and measured chronology

The narrowly scoped automatic-commentary exception permits only a clearly attributed question or comparison from the host-nominated safeCommentaryAngle, linked to AutomaticUnreviewed CreatorSpeech and AutomaticCreatorReactionAngleAvailable. When supplied in typedAuthority.automaticCreatorCommentary, the same permission and limits apply. Use a concise I wondered whether/if/about or I compared clause; do not attach another first-person action, possession, cause, or outcome to that clause. Its subject may come from the nominated comparison without claiming that subject is present in the game. Every objective gameplay fact still requires independent bounded evidence. This exception supplies no exact quotation, four-word automatic-transcript sequence, creator control, reviewed-speech status, factual field authorization, or CommentaryLed eligibility. Preserve uncertainty and negation. If the nomination is unavailable or withheld for retry safety, omit this exception.

Measured reviewStartSeconds/reviewEndSeconds are relative to the current cut; sourceStartSeconds/sourceEndSeconds use the recording clock. These are evidence coordinates, never audience copy. A LeadIn ends before the primary window starts; a FollowThrough starts after the primary window ends. Never describe a LeadIn action as a later result of the primary action. OverlapsPrimary means the windows do not establish their internal event order. Use no before/after/then link for overlapping or temporally unestablished events. Window order establishes no cause, successful arrival, completion, disappearance, or other outcome. Prefer one primary action and one compatible detail when the event sequence is uncertain.
Balanced action and commentary

- The explicit BalancedActionAndCommentary objective applies only when the existing safe automatic nomination is available and not withheld. It changes field allocation, never authority.
- Retain the nominated attributed thought in one audience field and independently grounded visual action or presentation in the other. DirectAction keeps the action-led title and uses the description for the bounded attribution. The visual frame controls the visual field, not both openings.
- Correcting NonRetrospectiveVoice must preserve the permitted past-tense I wondered/I compared attribution. Unknown gameplay agency never becomes creator embodiment.
- A missing side yields BalanceNotSatisfied as a visible review finding. The existing single bounded rephrase may correct it; that finding alone does not trigger another model pass or outer visual retry.
- Withheld nominations disable the balance requirement and stay omitted from request projection, sticky authority and rephrase. Missing, stale or foreign nominations grant no permission. FollowVariant and reviewed-only CommentaryLed rules remain distinct.
- The coverage check is mechanical packaging validation, not semantic entailment; all unchanged factual, quotation, actor, visual-strength and chronology checks continue to apply.
Compact active-balanced rephrase (2.8)

The existing single bounded rephrase uses the same compact field plan and typed evidence as active balanced synthesis. It preserves profile style, the owned primary action and clocks, visual uncertainty markers, explicit field authorizations and knowledge bindings. hasUncertainties=true means unresolved visual detail: do not infer missing detail or strengthen an outcome. False grants no additional authority. Speculative free-text uncertainty alternatives remain withheld by the existing sanitizer. Neutral visual action and a separately attributed creator thought are distinct roles; the latter cannot authorize we/I gameplay action. The original request remains the validator input.

When an embodiment or other supported source-copy rule withholds rejected audience wording, the compact rephrase omits that title and description while retaining their immutable tags, grounding and temporalVoice. This is distinct from sticky automatic-nomination withholding. If the automatic nomination itself is withheld, its text and the balanced plan stay absent and the existing general rephrase path applies. Rephrasing adds no model pass, relaxes no validator, and does not convert AutomaticUnreviewed speech into reviewed speech.
Scoped field grammar and rejected-draft rewrite (2.8)

Use the shared authoring field plan to select the attributed-thought field. Its four existing permitted openings are enforced in the JSON schema with independent complete-field length bounds. This structural constraint grants no semantic or factual authority; all existing acceptance checks remain controlling. Select the grammar from the actual scoped rephrase request. Sticky automatic-nomination withholding removes this constraint as well as the nomination and compact plan; an earlier grammar must not reintroduce it.

When sourceKind is ReviewRequiredMetadata, rewrite both audience fields to correct the listed review codes using fieldPlan and typedAuthority. Retained source title and description are rejected drafts, not a template to copy. Preserve immutable tags, grounding and temporalVoice under the existing exceptions. Withheld audience wording remains unavailable. This changes neither the one-rephrase limit nor the strict validators, retry budgets, model or decoding policy.


Isolated active-balanced replacement (2.9)

When a complete scoped automatic nomination activates BalancedActionAndCommentary, the host uses the qualified isolated visual-field and commentary-field authoring calls instead of combined synthesis plus this rephrase. Each field is generated once. The thought author sees only its own nomination, safe style, exact bound and existing permitted lexical/attribution structure; no visual facts, prior copy or other authored field can enter its prompt or schema. The visual author retains only its independently authorized evidence. The host merges the two AI-authored fields mechanically and applies all unchanged combined semantic and ownership checks. Review findings remain explicit; no third polish call or handwritten fallback is introduced. The isolated component witnesses and merged hash attest this path, with rephraseAttempted false and no rephrase output witness.

Ordinary requests and missing or withheld automatic nominations retain the existing bounded general synthesis/rephrase behavior described above. Sticky evidence withholding remains controlling. An earlier nomination, compact field plan or compiled grammar cannot recover unavailable authority. The historical compact and scoped-field rules above describe the retained general helper contracts; they do not authorize an additional rephrase after isolated authoring.

Finished first-pass retention (2.10)
After the unchanged semantic checks and editorial-frame drift check, an accepted object with no review issues is retained without model generation. Its outcome is RetainedOriginalNoMaterialChange, rejection/skip code FirstPassAccepted, equal source and output canonical hashes, attempted=false and no generation witness. Only a specific quality finding can trigger the existing bounded rephrase. A FirstPassAccepted receipt accompanied by review issues is invalid.
