Replay Foundry Grounded Editorial Synthesis Recovery Pool Policy 1.5

Purpose

This policy preserves policy 1.4's immutable sampled recovery pool and extends
its activation to a third completed ordinary-pass rejection with a known typed
semantic code. It changes no model, grounding packet, perception pass, visual
sampling, evidence authority, metadata prompt, metadata schema, validator,
seed, or decoding parameter.

Bounded activation

- The pool activates after exactly three completed schema-valid ordinary greedy
  rejections when either ordinary passes 2 and 3 are byte-and-SHA-identical or
  ordinary pass 3 ends with a known retryable semantic rejection.
- Technical errors, UsageOrInputError, incomplete EOS, XGrammar failure,
  malformed output, unknown rejection, StrictOutputValidation, or incomplete
  attestation never activate the pool and fail closed immediately.
- Duplicate witnesses remain required and unchanged when passes 2 and 3 are
  identical. A nonduplicate semantic-exhaustion activation emits no duplicate
  witness and is independently proven by its three rejected-pass attestations.

Cross-draft retry source withholding

- A completed CrossDraftTitleContamination rejection activates primary-only
  synthesis evidence exactly as before.
- The immediately rejected title, description, tags, grounding, and temporal
  voice are not rendered into the next retry messages. The retry receives no
  assistant audience-copy target and no compact copy envelope.
- The rejected pass ordinal and canonical-JSON SHA-256 remain attested with the
  source-selection reason CrossDraftRejectedAudienceCopyWithheld. This witness
  identifies the discarded source and does not claim that its text was rendered.
- Every non-CrossDraft ordinary retry retains the prior policy's exact immediate
  assistant JSON and correction-envelope behavior.

Conditional immutable recovery source

- When any ordinary pass failed CrossDraftTitleContamination and primary-only
  synthesis evidence is active, every recovery candidate withholds the pass-3
  audience copy. Its discarded source pass and hash remain attested with reason
  PrimaryOnlyCrossDraftAudienceCopyWithheld.
- That branch also withholds any compact or sticky title-copy envelope captured
  later in the ordinary passes; the cumulative typed rules and freshly derived
  sanitized authority remain available without repeating rejected wording.
- For every other recovery, the correction target remains the exact bounded
  canonical schema-valid JSON rejected on ordinary pass 1, with reason
  OriginalFirstRejected.
- Source selection is frozen before the pool. Messages, selected-primary
  evidence, correction state, rejected-rule state, authority state, and source
  witness remain identical for every candidate.
- A withheld or retained target is non-evidence and non-authority. It authorizes
  no fact, identity, wording, grounding, or grammatical repair.
- A sticky grammar target may be captured only when the actual typed rejection
  code is exactly NonRetrospectiveVoice.
- No rejected pool candidate is added to any later candidate's messages.

Authorized recovery configuration

- Trigger: BoundedSemanticRecoveryActivated.
- Logical pass ordinal: 4.
- Pool size: 4 serial batch-one candidates.
- Candidate seeds in ordinal order: 3407, 3408, 3409, 3410.
- Decoding: do_sample=true and num_beams=1.
- Cache: enabled.
- Temperature: 0.7.
- Top-p: 0.8.
- Top-k: 20.
- Maximum new tokens per candidate: 768.
- Structured decoding: a fresh batch-one XGrammar matcher for every candidate.

Typed semantic continuation

- Each candidate must reach bounded EOS, produce one complete canonical
  schema-valid JSON object, satisfy complete immutable-input attestation, and
  pass the unchanged parser and complete validator stack.
- A completed known semantic rejection may advance to the next frozen seed.
- Technical errors, UsageOrInputError, incomplete EOS, XGrammar failure,
  malformed output, unknown rejection, StrictOutputValidation, or attestation
  mismatch terminate immediately and fail closed.
- The first fully valid candidate wins. Four semantic rejections fail closed.
- No unconstrained fallback, semantic repair, text repair, conjugation,
  validator weakening, dynamic seed, beam search, perception rerun, grounding
  rerun, or additional logical synthesis pass is permitted.

Attestation

- Success and typed failure provenance bind the policy identity, activation
  class through duplicate-witness presence, source reason, discarded-or-retained
  source pass and canonical-JSON hash, canonical messages, rendered prompt,
  input tokens, output, validation result, and module hashes.
- Current output readers accept a pool without duplicate witnesses only when
  three ordinary completed rejections are attested and the third is a frozen
  known retryable semantic code.
- Historical policy 1.4 artifacts retain their duplicate-only activation rule.
