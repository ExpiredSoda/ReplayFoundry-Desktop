Replay Foundry Grounded Editorial Synthesis Recovery Pool Policy 1.9

Purpose

This policy retains policy 1.8's bounded semantic recovery pool, immutable
evidence, four fixed seeds, first-valid selection, and strict factual boundary.
It removes TerminalTitlePeriod from semantic rejection because exactly one
sentence-style final ASCII period is now canonicalized before semantic
validation. Punctuation formatting is not a reason to spend another model pass.

Recovery

- Three ordinary greedy synthesis attempts remain bounded by the same evidence.
- The recovery pool remains four sequential batch-one candidates with seeds
  3407, 3408, 3409, and 3410, temperature 0.7, top-p 0.8, and top-k 20.
- Every completed candidate receives the same immutable messages, typed
  authority, and full factual validators. The first fully valid candidate wins.
- Technical, malformed, unattested, unknown, and exhausted paths fail closed.

Title punctuation

- One final ASCII period is removed by the canonical metadata validator without
  changing any word. It is neither a semantic rejection nor a recovery-pool
  retry reason.
- All other title, evidence, grounding, language, actor, diversity, and
  chronology rules remain unchanged.

No game-specific behavior

- The policy contains no game, character, level, scene, or example title.
- XGrammar constrains JSON structure; it does not perform prose editing or
  gameplay interpretation.

Attestation

- Current output provenance binds this policy identity, the exact retryable
  semantic-code set, seeds, messages, prompts, token IDs, and completed outputs.
- Policy 1.8 and earlier remain readable under their original contracts.
