Replay Foundry Grounded Editorial Synthesis Recovery Pool Policy 1.7

Purpose

This policy preserves policy 1.6's bounded recovery pool and removes one
conflicting synthesis instruction. A saved creator-voice preference remains
user configuration, but the model-facing voice perspective is derived from the
typed actor authority and creator-experience relation for the selected visual
event.

Effective voice projection

- NeutralNoSubject remains NeutralNoSubject.
- CreatorFirstPerson is supplied to synthesis only for CreatorControlled with
  CreatorActed, CreatorAffected, or CreatorEncountered authority.
- CreatorFirstPerson paired with Unknown or Unestablished authority is projected
  to NeutralNoSubject for every ordinary and recovery synthesis candidate.
- Projection does not mutate the saved profile, infer creator control, promote
  gameplay or camera viewpoint into evidence, weaken validation, or repair
  generated text.
- The same selected-event authority deterministically produces the same
  model-facing profile for all retries and recovery candidates.

Preserved bounded recovery

- Pool activation, rejected-copy withholding, source selection, immutable
  messages, typed semantic continuation, and fail-closed behavior remain those
  of policy 1.6.
- The pool remains four serial batch-one candidates with seeds 3407, 3408, 3409,
  and 3410; do_sample=true; num_beams=1; use_cache=true; temperature=0.7;
  top_p=0.8; top_k=20; maximum 768 new tokens; and a fresh XGrammar matcher for
  every candidate.
- The first fully valid candidate wins. Technical, malformed, incomplete,
  unknown, StrictOutputValidation, or attestation failures terminate
  immediately. Four known semantic rejections fail closed.
- No unconstrained fallback, semantic repair, text repair, dynamic seed, beam
  search, perception rerun, grounding rerun, or additional logical synthesis
  pass is permitted.

Attestation

- Current output provenance binds this policy identity and the existing exact
  synthesis message, rendered prompt, input-token, output, validation, source,
  and module witnesses.
- Policy 1.6 artifacts retain their original unsanitized profile-context reader
  and validation contract.
