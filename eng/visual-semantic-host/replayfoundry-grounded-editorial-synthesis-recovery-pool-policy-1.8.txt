Replay Foundry Grounded Editorial Synthesis Recovery Pool Policy 1.8

Purpose

This policy preserves policy 1.7's bounded recovery pool and separates one
interface-attribution rejection from the broader unsupported-interpretation
rule. A retry can now correct the relationship between readable interface text
and physical scene objects without discarding otherwise literal copy.

Interface-attribution correction

- UnsupportedInterfaceAttribution is a known completed semantic rejection.
- Retry guidance keeps screen-space menus, HUD elements, and readable interface
  text separate from physical objects in the scene.
- A retry may describe a menu or overlay directly as an interface element.
- A retry may not claim that interface lettering appeared on, in, or was shown
  by a screen, display, monitor, sign, or billboard unless the marked primary
  visual draft explicitly identifies that physical source.
- This correction does not authorize readable wording, consumer-platform
  identity, a physical display source, intent, emotion, or unseen causality.

Preserved bounded recovery

- Pool activation, rejected-copy withholding, source selection, immutable
  messages, typed semantic continuation, effective voice projection, and
  fail-closed behavior remain those of policy 1.7.
- The pool remains four serial batch-one candidates with seeds 3407, 3408, 3409,
  and 3410; do_sample=true; num_beams=1; use_cache=true; temperature=0.7;
  top_p=0.8; top_k=20; maximum 768 new tokens; and a fresh XGrammar matcher for
  every candidate.
- The first fully valid candidate wins. Technical, malformed, incomplete,
  unknown, StrictOutputValidation, or attestation failures terminate
  immediately. Four known semantic rejections fail closed.
- No unconstrained fallback, semantic repair, text repair, dynamic seed, beam
  search, perception rerun, grounding rerun, or additional logical synthesis
  pass is permitted.

Attestation

- Current output provenance binds this policy identity and the existing exact
  synthesis message, rendered prompt, input-token, output, validation, source,
  and module witnesses.
- Policy 1.7 artifacts retain their original combined unsupported-interpretation
  rejection and reader contract.
