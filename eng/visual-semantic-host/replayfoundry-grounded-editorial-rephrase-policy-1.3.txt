Replay Foundry Grounded Editorial Rephrase Policy 1.3

Purpose

This policy retains policy 1.2's bounded text-only polish and rejected-language
recovery pass. It extends the typed final-language correction to unsupported
creator embodiment so an unidentified visible person cannot be recast as the
creator when the creator-experience relation is unestablished.

Accepted-copy polish

- An already accepted canonical metadata object may receive one text-only
  polish pass exactly as in policy 1.2.
- A valid materially changed candidate replaces its title and description. An
  identical candidate or completed semantic rejection keeps the accepted
  original.

Rejected-language recovery

- Recovery is available only after every completed recovery-pool candidate was
  rejected by one of these known language rules: NonRetrospectiveVoice,
  UnsupportedCreatorEmbodiment, ThirdPersonCreatorFraming,
  FirstPersonTitleSubject, GenericOpening, or IncompleteTitle.
- The source is one completed schema-valid recovery candidate, its exact typed
  rejection code, and a sanitized authority summary re-derived from the
  unchanged grounding packet. No sampled rejection becomes evidence.
- One batch-one greedy XGrammar-constrained text-only pass may rewrite only
  titleBody and description. Tags, grounding binding IDs, and temporalVoice
  remain equivalent as parsed JSON values.
- For ThirdPersonCreatorFraming under CreatorControlled plus CreatorActed, the
  correction must remove generic human labels and narrate the supported
  controlled action retrospectively as I or my. It may not transfer any action
  absent from the typed primary visual authority.
- For ThirdPersonCreatorFraming without that exact authority pair, the
  correction must remove generic human-label openings and use a supported
  retrospective action, passive result, setting, or state without inventing a
  creator relationship.
- For UnsupportedCreatorEmbodiment when the typed creator-experience relation
  is Unestablished, the correction must remove I, we, my, our, and every
  generic human subject. It must begin both titleBody and description with a
  supported completed action, visible result, setting, or state and must not
  convert the visible person's body, weapon, dialogue, or action into the
  creator's experience.
- For UnsupportedCreatorEmbodiment under CreatorControlled plus CreatorActed,
  only the supported controlled action may use retrospective I or my; another
  person's body detail, dialogue, emotion, or action remains neutral.
- The candidate must pass the same strict metadata, grounding,
  creator-authority, audience-copy, terminal-period, and reroll-diversity
  validators. A fully valid candidate is recorded as
  RecoveredRejectedLanguage. Any further semantic or technical failure remains
  terminal.

Preserved bounds

- The pass receives no video, performs no perception or knowledge retrieval,
  and uses seed 0, beam count 1, enabled offloaded KV cache, and the existing
  768-token ceiling.
- No unconstrained fallback, string substitution, conjugation, deterministic
  semantic repair, extra retry, new fact, changed citation, changed tag,
  changed creator authority, or changed evidence scope is permitted.

Attestation

- Current output provenance records the policy identity, whether the pass was
  attempted and applied, its bounded outcome, source and output canonical JSON
  hashes, rejection code when applicable, and exact message, rendered prompt,
  input-token, and raw-output witnesses.
- Policies 1.0 through 1.2 remain readable under their original output
  contracts.
