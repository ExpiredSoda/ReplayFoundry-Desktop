Replay Foundry Grounded Editorial Rephrase Policy 1.7

Purpose

This policy retains policy 1.6's bounded text-only polish, typed language
recovery, literal-action recovery, retrospective grammar recovery, and direct
source-versus-correction failure comparison. It removes a false conflict
between creator authority and natural neutral descriptions of another or
unidentified visible person.

Neutral person recovery

- Tags are descriptive metadata, not grammatical narration. First-person
  pronoun matching for creator embodiment inspects titleBody and description
  only; a retained tag such as The Last of Us cannot authorize or falsely
  trigger creator embodiment.
- Unknown or OtherPerson actor authority without CreatorActed relation permits
  a neutral human subject such as "a person" when the typed primary visual
  literally supports that visible subject and action.
- Neutral permission does not permit the audience-role labels player,
  character, streamer, creator, or camera wearer, does not invent I or we, and
  does not transfer another person's body, weapon, dialogue, emotion, or action
  into the creator's experience.
- Every accepted action remains grammatically retrospective. Simple-present,
  command, infinitive, and gerund action openings remain invalid.

Rejected-copy recovery

- ThirdPersonCreatorFraming and UnsupportedCreatorEmbodiment corrections use
  the neutral-person rule only when the typed authority qualifies. Otherwise
  policy 1.6's creator-controlled and creator-experience rules remain exact.
- One batch-one greedy XGrammar-constrained text-only pass may rewrite only
  titleBody and description. Tags, grounding binding IDs, and temporalVoice
  remain equivalent as parsed JSON values.
- The candidate must pass every strict grounding, actor, audience,
  action-strength, language, and diversity validator. Any further semantic or
  technical failure remains terminal.

Preserved bounds

- No video, perception, knowledge retrieval, unconstrained fallback, string
  substitution, deterministic semantic repair, extra retry, new fact, changed
  citation, changed tag, changed creator authority, or changed evidence scope.
- Seed 0, beam count 1, offloaded KV cache, and the 768-token ceiling remain.

Attestation

- Current output provenance retains the source canonical JSON hash, policy
  identity, rejection code, outcome, output hash, and exact message, prompt,
  input-token, and output witnesses.
- Policies 1.0 through 1.6 remain readable under their original contracts.
