Replay Foundry Grounded Editorial Rephrase Policy 1.6

Purpose

This policy retains policy 1.5's bounded text-only polish, typed language
recovery, and literal-action recovery. It makes retrospective grammar recovery
explicit and makes a failed correction directly comparable with its source.

Accepted-copy polish

- An already accepted canonical metadata object may receive one text-only
  polish pass exactly as in policy 1.5.
- A valid materially changed candidate replaces its title and description. An
  identical candidate or completed semantic rejection keeps the accepted
  original.

Rejected-copy recovery

- Recovery remains limited to completed, schema-valid rejection codes frozen by
  this policy.
- NonRetrospectiveVoice requires both audience fields to be grammatically
  retrospective. The title must not begin with a command, bare infinitive,
  simple-present verb, or gerund, and present-tense action narration is
  prohibited.
- CreatorControlled plus CreatorActed authority may use I or we followed by an
  unmistakable past-tense action. Otherwise correction remains neutral, does not
  invent creator embodiment, and begins with a supported past-tense action,
  completed visible result, or visible state.
- UnsupportedMentalState and UnsupportedCreatorEmbodiment retain policy 1.5's
  audience-copy withholding and typed-authority reconstruction rules.
- One batch-one greedy XGrammar-constrained text-only pass may rewrite only
  titleBody and description. Tags, grounding binding IDs, and temporalVoice
  remain equivalent as parsed JSON values.
- The candidate must pass every existing strict grounding, actor, audience,
  action-strength, language, and diversity validator. Any further semantic or
  technical failure remains terminal.

Failure comparison

- A completed rejected rephrase carries the bounded source title and
  description and the bounded rephraser title and description in its local
  technical failure message. This changes diagnostics only, not acceptance.

Preserved bounds

- No video, perception, knowledge retrieval, unconstrained fallback, string
  substitution, deterministic semantic repair, extra retry, new fact, changed
  citation, changed tag, changed creator authority, or changed evidence scope.
- Seed 0, beam count 1, offloaded KV cache, and the 768-token ceiling remain.

Attestation

- Current output provenance retains the original rejected canonical JSON hash,
  policy identity, rejection code, outcome, output hash, and exact message,
  prompt, input-token, and output witnesses.
- Policies 1.0 through 1.5 remain readable under their original contracts.
