# Community standards

Replay Foundry aims to be a thoughtful, welcoming project for creators and contributors of every background and experience level.

Be respectful, assume good intent while discussing evidence, criticize ideas rather than people, and make space for others to participate. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior undermines a safe, productive community. Conduct concerns should be reported privately to the repository owner; reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential to the extent practical.
